Teaching

Cheesy Correlations and Causality

Teaching module · Florence, Italy · April 2026–present

A teaching module for social science courses. The data comes from a fictional cheese store and works for all analyses, from bachelor to master level. The dataset covers the impact of COVID-19 and the farmer protests, and contains many hooks and side quests to keep students engaged. It comes with a teaching manual that describes the hooks and hypotheses, plus code for R, Stata, and SPSS.

The dataset is available on request: Toine.Fiselier@EUI.eu.

Thesis coaching

Jouw Scriptiecoach · The Netherlands · June 2024–present

Individual thesis coaching for students in Political Science, Psychology, Communication Science, Economics, and Business Administration. I specialise in:

  • Statistical analyses: choosing the right method, running analyses in SPSS, Stata, or R, and interpreting the results.
  • Research design: building a coherent and sound research setup, including operationalisation, sampling, and data collection.
  • Academic writing: structuring arguments, writing clearly, and meeting academic standards.

Teaching assistant

Department of Political Science, Radboud University, Nijmegen · September 2020–July 2022

Courses taught:

  • Political Science Research Methods: introductory methods course covering research design, data collection, and quantitative and qualitative analysis.
  • Project 1: Explanations for Political Attitudes and Behavior in the Netherlands: students design and carry out an original research project on Dutch political attitudes and behavior.
  • Project 3: Comparative Governance: advanced research project on comparative analysis of governance across political systems.
  • Sociology, Philosophy, and Ethics of Research: the philosophical foundations of social science research, covering epistemology, research ethics, and the sociology of knowledge.
  • Bachelor thesis supervision: individual supervision of students writing their final thesis.

Alongside teaching, I helped write policy reports for the Dutch Electoral Council and the Netherlands Institute for Social Research (SCP).
