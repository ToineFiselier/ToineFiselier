Teaching

Cheesy Correlations and Causality

Development and design · Florence, Italy · April 2026–present

A statistics teaching module for social science courses. The data comes from a fictional cheese market and works for all analyses, from bachelor to master level. The dataset covers the impact of COVID-19 and the farmer protests, and contains many hooks and side quests to keep students engaged. It comes with a teaching manual that describes the hooks and hypotheses, plus code for R, Stata, and SPSS.

The dataset is available on request: toinefiselier@gmail.com.

Thesis coaching

Jouw Scriptiecoach · Santpoort-Noord, The Netherlands · June 2024–present

Individual thesis coaching for students in Political Science, Psychology, Medicine, Communication Science, Economics, and Business Administration. I specialise in:

  • Statistical analyses: choosing the right method, running analyses in SPSS, Stata, or R, and interpreting the results.
  • Research design: building a coherent and sound research setup, including operationalisation, sampling, and data collection.
  • Academic writing: structuring arguments, writing clearly, and meeting academic standards.
