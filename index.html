Postdoctoral Researcher, ESSEC Business School, Cergy, France

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at ESSEC Business School in Cergy, France, working with Professor Aurélien Colson on the R-S3i project. This two-year project, funded by CY Initiative, studies resilience to foreign influence, interference, and intimidation in European democracies. I coordinate the project and research how citizens respond to foreign interference and elite cues.

I wrote my PhD in Political and Social Sciences at the European University Institute (EUI) in Fiesole, Italy, supervised by Professor Filip Kostelka. My dissertation, Beyond the Alarm: Three Perspectives on Polarization, examines when ideological and affective polarization are harmful or beneficial for democratic legitimacy. My defense is on 2 November 2026.

Before the EUI, I was a teaching and research assistant at Radboud University in Nijmegen, where I also completed my MSc in Comparative Politics and BSc in Political Science. I also hold a BA in Music Management from HKU University of the Arts, Utrecht.

Research interests

Substantive: democratic legitimacy, political support, polarization (ideological and affective), foreign influence and interference, elite cues and opinion formation, democratic resilience, political conflict, democratic erosion, political behaviour.

Methodological: causal inference, survey experiments, panel data analysis, public opinion research.
