PhD Researcher in Political Science, European University Institute, Fiesole, Italy

I am a PhD Researcher in Political Science at the European University Institute (EUI) in Fiesole, Italy, where I am supervised by Professor Filip Kostelka. My research sits at the intersection of polarization and political support. I study how different types of polarization (affective and ideological) affect democratic legitimacy, and under which conditions they are harmful or beneficial for democracies.

Before joining the EUI, I worked as a teaching and research assistant at Radboud University in Nijmegen, where I also completed my MSc in Comparative Politics and BSc in Political Science. I also hold a BA in Music Management from HKU University of the Arts, Utrecht.

Research interests

Substantive: democratic legitimacy, political support, polarization (ideological and affective), representation, partisanship, perceptions of electoral integrity, political behavior.

Methodological: causal inference, research design, survey experiments, panel data analysis, public opinion research, semi-structured interviews, text-coding.
